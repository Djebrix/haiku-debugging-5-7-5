One haiku about debugging, in the strict 5-7-5 syllable shape: a breakpoint waiting at 3am, log lines scrolling past midnight, and a last line that turns the hunt back on the hunter. `main.py` (Python 3 stdlib only) writes the haiku to `entry.txt`.

> Breakpoint waits till three
> the log lines scroll past midnight
> and the bug was me
